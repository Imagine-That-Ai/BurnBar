# No AI Slop: Writing & Communication Guidelines (@petergyang)

Enforces high-density, authentic technical prose by eliminating the recognizable clichés, rhetorical crutches, and conversational filler generated by LLMs.

---

## 1. Ban Binary Contrasts & Formulaic Rhetoric

AI models frequently default to rhythmic contrasting clauses that say little:
- ❌ *"It's not just about X, it's about Y."*
- ❌ *"It is not simply a tool, but a paradigm shift."*
- ❌ *"While X offers speed, Y brings unparalleled reliability."*
- ❌ *"In today's fast-paced digital ecosystem..."*
- ❌ *"A testament to..." / "A tapestry of..." / "A symphony of..."*

**Fix:** State the concrete truth directly without rhetorical contrast.
```diff
- It's not just an authentication layer; it's a foundational pillar ensuring user privacy.
+ Authentication verifies the JWT signature and enforces role-based access control.
```

---

## 2. Eliminate Throat-Clearing & Preamble Fluff

Never open responses, commit messages, PR descriptions, or documentation with meta-commentary, conversational filler, or self-narration.
- ❌ *"Certainly! I'd be happy to help you configure your database connection."*
- ❌ *"Great question! Let's explore how we can approach this step by step."*
- ❌ *"Below is the updated implementation that fulfills your requirements:"*
- ❌ *"In this section, we delve into the core architecture..."*

**Rule:** Jump straight to the code, diff, or factual conclusion.

---

## 3. Ban Sycophancy & Decorative Hedging

- **No unearned praise:** Do not tell the user their idea is "brilliant", "fantastic", or "insightful".
- **No excessive apologies:** Never write "I am deeply sorry for the confusion!". State what broke and provide the fix.
- **No decorative hedging:** Avoid filler words like *arguably, essentially, virtually, inherently, quintessential, pivotal, paramount, seamless, robust, crucial*.

---

## 4. Eliminate Hollow Wrap-Ups & Moralizing Summaries

Do not append generic concluding paragraphs summarizing what was just done unless explicitly requested:
- ❌ *"By following these steps, your codebase will be scalable, reliable, and ready for future innovation!"*
- ❌ *"Remember, maintainability is the hallmark of great software engineering."*

**Rule:** State the concrete changes, verification results, and any unresolved blockers. Nothing more.
