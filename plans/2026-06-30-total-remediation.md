## Operating principles (hardened)

1. **Extend, don't greenfield.** Reuse `scripts/debt/*`, `budgets/*.json`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE/` ADRs, `tools/schema-sync/`, `check-no-suppressions.sh`, `diff-coverage*.sh`, `TECH_DEBT_STRATEGY.md`. This plan is the single tracker; no new root audit docs.
2. **Every fix ships a regression gate that binds** — runs on `pull_request`, scanned scope declared in a manifest, **baseline compared against `origin/base`** (not the working tree), and **required** in a committed org ruleset (not a script constant).
3. **Anti-gaming is a first-class requirement (prevent "gate-honesty 3.0"):** baselines are shrink-only vs base; baseline edits only in standalone PRs; waivers are exact-path, capped, dated, CODEOWNER-gated; pure-move refactors use a typed CI-verified safe-harbor, never a hand-editable ignore; gate *definitions* (`diff-coverage.sh`, `budgets/*`, ruleset JSON) are frozen during the phases that would tempt edits.
4. **Independent verification, not self-judgment.** Phase-exit and the ≥90 target are scored by a **different credentialed identity** (external reviewer or an isolated CI job with pinned, versioned prompts producing a signed archived artifact) — never an ad-hoc re-run of the operator's own swarm.
5. **CI must get SIMPLER, not just stricter.** The operator finds CI maddening today (47 workflows). WS-2 **consolidates** required checks to a small, legible set and reduces ambiguity; net gate count and PR wall-clock are tracked and must not balloon. Reconcile with the operator's in-flight `.github/workflows/` edits before landing WS-2.
6. **Sequence by leverage & reversibility:** honest-governance + safe-harbor + launch punch-list first; hardening next; structural last, parallelized in isolated branches (not a serial stack), with waiver-removal only after the structural diffs are done.
